# Sweetroll FM: track credits

Sweetroll FM, the radio station after Radio Sovngarde on the mouse wheel's dial, plays these tracks. Every one is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) by its author, as its source page states (a page listing CC0 among several licenses counts: CC0 is the one used here). Attribution isn't required, but the authors are credited anyway.

`scripts/encode-music.ps1` reads the table below: it downloads each track from its Download link into `music/sources/` (git-ignored) and encodes it to `music/sweetrollfm/<File>` (44.1 kHz stereo xWMA), the name `protocol/cpp/radio.h` plays it by. Lengths are approximate. Nothing here comes from San Andreas or Skyrim.
