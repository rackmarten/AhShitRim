## [0.1.0] - unreleased

The first release, Thane of Grove Street: CJ plays Skyrim from the Helgen cart to the Greybeards, on foot and in every land and air vehicle San Andreas has, with its guns, fists, cheat codes, wanted level, HUD and radar, radio and his own voice. Protocol v35. Install: [docs/INSTALL.md](docs/INSTALL.md); what doesn't work yet: the README's known issues. Sweetroll FM's tracks come in 0.1.1.

### Added

- San Andreas's whole PC vehicle keymap (protocol v23), on its own default keys where Skyrim doesn't need them. In a vehicle Left Alt and Right Ctrl fire like the left mouse button, Left Ctrl and numpad 0 like the right one (the BMX's and mountain bike's bunny hop, the Hunter's machine gun, the Hydra's missiles), the middle mouse button looks behind (Q and E together do too; on foot it still swears), V and Home change the camera distance, 2 and numpad + are San Andreas's sub-mission key with numpad 5 (the Hydra's and every plane's landing gear, the hydraulics' jump, the crop duster's and Stuntplane's smoke), Caps Lock is the horn in the modern layout too, and Insert and 4 tune the next radio station, Delete and R the one before. W and S held together do San Andreas's burnout, the wheels spinning in place while A and D turn the car (they used to cancel out). In the classic layout Right Ctrl fires as well as being the handbrake, as San Andreas binds it. The Hydra's heat seeker locks on to Skyrim's dragons, mammoths, giants, centurions and riders: hold Space (its lock-on, San Andreas's R1) with the target in view until the marker turns red, then fire the missiles; San Andreas's own lock only looks for its vehicles. The Hunter's rockets lock on and home the same way, unlike San Andreas's, where they're always unguided; without a lock they fly straight. Looking left, right or behind in a vehicle no longer puts Skyrim's camera a vehicle length too far back: it used the active camera's source, which stays at the follow spot behind the vehicle while only San Andreas's final camera matrix moves beside it (7.5 m apart on an NRG-500), so it now takes the final matrix. San Andreas logs a plane's landing gear going up and down. The heat seeker's lock-on marker (on foot and in the Hydra) is now San Andreas's own, as it draws a flying vehicle's lock: the green ring with four ticks and an inner ring turning once a second, outlined in black, turning red once locked, the same size on the screen however far the target is. In the Hydra's and the Hunter's first-person camera the white rocket-sight frame shows in the middle of the screen, as in San Andreas. The README's keys table now lists every vehicle action, and corrects two: the Hunter's left mouse button fires its rockets and the right one its machine gun, and the Hydra's left button drops flares. San Andreas's F5 track skip and its mouse look stay out (F5 is Skyrim's quick save, the right mouse button the second weapon). Game-free tests for every new key.
- AIR before a plane's name (AIRAT400, AIRBEAGLE, AIRANDROM, AIRHYDRA...) spawns it already flying, 150 m up with CJ at the controls, so the big planes that can't take off from Skyrim's rough ground can be flown at all. AIR before anything that isn't a plane spawns it on the ground as usual.
- Two more of San Andreas's cheats. IAVENJQ (or STINGLIKEABEE), mega punch: CJ's fists, brass knuckles and parachute kill whoever they hit and throw them far (giants, mammoths and dragons just die, and essential people kneel unless NOMERCY is on). BSXSGGC (or BUBBLECARS): a vehicle CJ's vehicle hits floats away, the vehicles he spawns included.
- Quests as San Andreas missions, and the line AhShitRim is named after (protocol v24). A quest starting (once it has a journal entry, as Skyrim announces it) shows its name as San Andreas's mission title, bottom right in gold, and a quest completed shows "MISSION PASSED!" and "RESPECT +" with San Andreas's own mission passed tune, instead of Skyrim's quest notifications, whoever has the player. Objectives and failed quests stay Skyrim's. A Miscellaneous quest done (a favour, a delivery, A Lovely Letter) is San Andreas's odd job: MISSION PASSED! alone with the jingle, no RESPECT + and no title when it starts, so fetch quests don't spam; Skyrim only reports their objectives, so one counts once its objective is completed and the quest ends right after. `[Missions] MiscPassed=0` in `AhShitRim.ini` turns that off; the launcher and `deploy-local.ps1` now keep that and `KeyLayout` when they rewrite the ini. They come one at a time: a passed mission before the next one's title, and only once its tune is over. On a new game, right after Ralof's "Hey, you. You're finally awake." in the Helgen cart, CJ says "Ah shit, here we go again." in his own voice, the first line of San Andreas's intro voice-over, with San Andreas's subtitle. Both sounds are San Andreas's own, played by its own routines and ids from your install (the tune as its mission scripts play it, the line as its intro script does), through a new sound cue counter in the input; San Andreas is heard for them even while Skyrim has the player, and Skyrim's music pauses meanwhile. Game-free tests for which quests count, the order and timing, the intro's trigger and the cues.
- San Andreas's own particles for the vehicles and the jetpack (protocol v26). The jetpack's two jets burn with San Andreas's flames, small and orange at rest and longer and blue while CJ thrusts; every car smokes from its exhausts as in San Andreas (more when revving in first or second gear, fading as it speeds up); nitro shoots its flames out of the exhausts while it burns and puffs its cooler as it charges again; a helicopter raises dust under its rotor near the ground; the Rhino's cannon fires with San Andreas's tank smoke and flash, and the Hunter's guns flash. All of it uses San Andreas's numbers from `models/effects.fxp` and the sprites in the player's own `models/effectsPC.txd`, drawn as camera-facing quads like the damage smoke.
- San Andreas's pickups in Skyrim's world (protocol v27, co-save version 5). Hearts, body armour, parachutes, weapons and wads of gold float and spin at spots across Skyrim, drawn with San Andreas's own models, and CJ takes one by walking into it, with San Andreas's sound: a heart fills his health, armour his armour bar, gold goes in Skyrim's purse and a weapon comes with San Andreas's street ammo. Parachutes wait on high spots for a base jump: the summit of the Throat of the World, High Hrothgar's terrace, the top of Bleak Falls Barrow, the roof of Dragonsreach, Arcwind Point, Forelhost, the Shrine of Azura, the College of Winterhold, Mount Anthor and Darklight Tower. Every hold has armour at two or three spots. They're drawn at twice San Andreas's size, to be seen in Skyrim's wide world. Taken ones come back as in San Andreas (30 s, armour and the rocket launcher and minigun after 6 minutes) once CJ is 10 m away; three stashes of gold are one-offs, gone for good, and the save remembers them. When CJ is down to a quarter of his health a heart turns up in front of him, at most once every 3 minutes. The jetpack CJ takes off now lies where it fell as a pickup, and walking back into it puts it on again.
- Running people over, and followers riding along (protocol v28). CJ's vehicle hits Skyrim's people, horses and creatures: a hit hurts by how fast it was (a car at 70 km/h kills an early bandit, a bike hurts less, the Rhino more) through Skyrim's own hit, so the kill, the bounty for running down a guard and everyone's anger are Skyrim's, and throws them along the way the car went. A person barely slows the car; a giant, a mammoth or a dragon stops it dead and dents it. CJ says one of his crash lines when he hits someone, at most every 4 seconds. When CJ gets in, his followers take the free passenger seats (Lydia rides in the Sandking's front seat, a bike's pillion sits behind him), drawn seated and carried along, and get out beside their doors when he does. Children and the riders' passengers aren't hit; passengers don't shoot.
- San Andreas's HUD sounds, from your own San Andreas install (protocol v30). The help box beeps as San Andreas's does when a message comes up, which is the sound of a cheat going on or off, a weapon skill going up and Ammu-Nation turning you away; buying at Ammu-Nation plays San Andreas's purchase sound, and taking gold off a body or out of a chest its money pickup sound. Skyrim sends any of San Andreas's HUD sounds through one `FrontendSound` message, which the pickups share.
- Skyrim's people can wreck CJ's vehicle (protocol v31). While he drives, arrows, blows, spells and blasts that reach the vehicle hit its shape (San Andreas's own collision box) instead of CJ inside, and every hostile's attack damages it through San Andreas: about 35 arrows or a dozen power attacks set a car on fire, four blows of a giant's club do it, and it smokes, burns and blows up as San Andreas's own cars do. A closed vehicle keeps all of Skyrim's damage off CJ and his passengers; on a bike, a bicycle or a quad he and his pillion take half and the bike half. Blasts never hurt anyone in a vehicle, CJ's own rockets and the Rhino's shell included, and the Rhino shrugs off arrows and blasts as San Andreas's does. Arrows stop at a car's body. CJ says one of his crash lines when his car gets hit, at most every 4 seconds, shared with the lines for running people over. When he's wanted and stops, the nearest guard puts his weapon away and runs round to the driver's door (to CJ on a bike) to bust him, giving up if he's stuck and letting the next one try. Nobody busts CJ while he's getting in or out, as in San Andreas, and giants don't run for the door. `scripts/hit-test.ps1` checks it all from the logs with nobody at the keys.
- Fist fights. Holding the right mouse button with fists or a melee weapon locks CJ on to the nearest enemy in front of him (anyone fighting him first, as San Andreas picks) and turns him to face them, so his punches land; letting go releases the lock. A punch or swing without it turns him to the nearest enemy within reach in front. San Andreas's small triangle marks the locked target over their head, green to red with their health; aiming a gun marks the enemy nearest the crosshair the same way, while the aim itself stays free. On foot CJ also bumps into Skyrim's people and creatures instead of walking through them, a giant or a mammoth by its size; followers still let him pass.
- A generator for the radar's map of Skyrim (`scripts/radar-map.ps1`, `protocol/tools/radar_map`): it reads your own Skyrim plugins in load order (mods that change the land included) and paints Tamriel flat in San Andreas's radar style, 32 pixels per cell (3808 x 3008): blue-grey water and rivers, greens for grass and forest, olive tundra, grey rock, pale snow, grey towns with white buildings, and roads as clean dark lines. It writes `radar-map.png` and `radar-map.json` (the world-to-pixel mapping and the plugins it came from) to `Documents\My Games\AhShitRim` in about 5 to 8 seconds, and only again when your plugins change. Nothing of Bethesda's is in the repo.
- San Andreas's radar, bottom left in San Andreas's spot and size, standing in for Skyrim's compass while CJ has the player. It turns with the camera, CJ's arrow in the middle and the N on the rim, and zooms out in a fast vehicle (180 m on foot to 350 m at 162 km/h, as San Andreas's; a plane's by its height). The map inside it is the generated San Andreas-style map of Skyrim (`radar-map.png` and `radar-map.json` in `Documents\My Games\AhShitRim`, or beside the plugin in `Data\SKSE\Plugins\AhShitRim`), with San Andreas's sea past its edges and plain grey without one; the launcher paints it before starting Skyrim (the release zip carries `radar_map.exe`), which takes a few seconds and nothing while it's up to date. The player's own map marker is San Andreas's red waypoint, quest targets (what the compass points to, through the door that leads there) are San Andreas's destination squares, up or down triangles when they're above or below CJ, Ammu-Nation is the gun icon at the blacksmiths' forges while it's near, and whoever is fighting the player is a small red square. Far targets sit on the rim. Interiors and other worldspaces show a dark radar with only the blips, and it goes away for cutscenes as San Andreas's does. `Compass=1` under `[Hud]` in `AhShitRim.ini` keeps Skyrim's compass up beside it; the launcher and `deploy-local.ps1` now keep that and `[Input] KeyLayout` when they rewrite the ini. The overlay draws shapes of any four corners now (turned sprites, triangles, the map's slices), not only boxes.
- The wanted level, mounted guards and Busted (protocol v22). Skyrim's bounty stays the real one; on top of it San Andreas's wanted stars (0 to 6) show in its HUD while CJ has the player. They come from heat, as San Andreas's from its chaos points (its thresholds, 50 to 2,400, with the sixth star at 3,600 so it takes as much as the fifth): each crime adds some (an assault 60, a murder 100, lesser crimes 15), so shooting a guard is one star, two guards killed two, four three, and six stars take a long rampage. Out of every pursuer's sight for 5 s they start to flash, then go one at a time (8 s for the last star, 18 s for the sixth); the bounty stays, and a guard who fights CJ later brings back a star, two for a murderer. Killing the witnesses wipes Skyrim's bounty but not the stars; paying the fine, Busted, Wasted and AEZAKMI end them, and petty crimes alone are a star at most. From 2 stars the hold's guards round about join, and the guards never run out: a director keeps up to 12 of ours after CJ, one per cooldown, never in view (behind him, or out of the city's barracks door), with a breather when they're all dead: riders on saddled horses from 3 stars, a captain and a guard wizard from 4, the hold's soldiers and a dragon from 5, two giants and a mammoth at 6 (the dragon, giants and mammoth only in the open world), all of them after CJ alone rather than each other. Guards hit CJ by the stars: 0.4 of their damage at 1 star, rising by 0.15 a star to all of it from 5 (a quarter when they fight him with none). CJ says San Andreas's own chased and arrested lines. Stopped in a car for a second with a guard at the driver's door, CJ is dragged out with San Andreas's jacked animation; stopped on a bike, bicycle or quad, he's knocked off. On foot Skyrim's guard dialogue stays (the fine, resisting, persuading), and only "go to jail" changes. Every Busted plays San Andreas's "busted", takes the bounty out of the gold (all of it if short), clears it, and CJ comes to outside the hold's barracks (Dragonsreach in Whiterun) with his weapons, no jail; nobody shoots him once he's arrested. OSRBLHH (TURNUPTHEHEAT) adds two stars, LJSPQK (BRINGITON) makes it six and ASNAEB (TURNDOWNTHEHEAT) clears them. A giant's club or a mammoth throws CJ as a car's blast does (San Andreas's fall and get-up) instead of leaving Skyrim's hidden player stuck in its ragdoll with the camera frozen. AEZAKMI (IDOASIPLEASE) clears every bounty and calls the guards off at once, then keeps the stars at 0 and puts back any bounty a crime adds. The stars are saved in a co-save record of their own (`ASWL`). New input flag `kInputBusted`.
- The people CJ kills come back. Any unique NPC the player kills while San Andreas has it (Ammu-Nation's blacksmiths, the main quest's people up to the Greybeards, anyone named) gets up an in-game hour later at their editor spot, inventory reset, once their body and that spot are out of CJ's sight. Works with NOMERCY too. The dead waiting are kept in the co-save (`ASRV`). Game-free tests for whose death counts, the timing, line of sight and the record.
- Conversations as San Andreas cutscenes (protocol v21). While CJ has the player, Skyrim's dialogue slides San Andreas's letterbox bars in and cuts to a two-shot of CJ and the speaker, then to the speaker over CJ's shoulder (pulled in by walls), keeping to one side of the two all conversation. CJ answers in his own voice, through San Andreas's audio: he greets when the dialogue opens, says a line of the topic's category (greet, agree, refuse, ask, threaten, joke, goodbye) whenever you pick one, cutting back to the two-shot, and says goodbye when you leave, never the same line twice in a row. Skyrim's subtitles, in dialogue and out of it, are drawn in San Andreas's subtitle style instead of Skyrim's, and a skipped line's goes at once. E or the left mouse button skips the speaker's line, and E on someone sitting (the Jarl on his throne) talks to them instead of offering the seat. The topic list stays Skyrim's. Which category a topic is comes from a table of Skyrim.esm topic IDs (the main quest up to the Greybeards and the generic town topics, classified by hand), generated by `protocol/tools/topic-table.mjs` from your own Skyrim.esm (it can also ask the homelab's local LLM through 8-ball), and a rule on the topic's text for anything else. CJ's lines per category start as his calm lines of the nearest San Andreas speech contexts; the voice picker's new "talk" menu picks them by ear. Game-free tests for the table, the rule, noticing a pick, the cuts and the shots.
- Skyrim's people have more to say about CJ's "magic", all vanilla lines in their own voices: guards have five lines when they hear shooting and five when CJ aims at them, civilians with no guard about remark on the spell cast near them, civilians aimed at pick from the magic greetings and a couple everyone has, the flamethrower brings the remarks on playing with fire, and the minigun, rockets and blasts are taken for a shout (the reactions to the player's Thu'um). Each line is said only by voice types that have its voice file, and nobody repeats a line until they've said the others that fit them.
- Ammu-Nation, gun rewards and looting without menus: guns, ammo and armour without the debug key. While San Andreas has the player, E on the blacksmith at Warmaiden's in Whiterun (Adrianne Avenicci or Ulfberth War-Bear), or the main blacksmith of Solitude, Windhelm, Riften, Markarth, Dawnstar or Falkreath, opens San Andreas's shop menu instead of Skyrim's dialogue (the help box says "E: Ammu-Nation (Adrianne Avenicci)"). It lists San Andreas's weapons by slot (pistols, shotguns, sub-machine guns, assault rifles, rifles, heavy weapons, thrown) and body armour, everything for sale at once and paid in gold: a pistol is 150, the minigun 15,000. A gun CJ already has sells its ammo instead, for less, in green; what he can't afford is grey. W/S (or the arrows) browse, E or the sprint key chooses, F or Enter goes back and leaves; CJ stands still meanwhile, and walking off closes it. The main quest gives guns, heavier as it goes: a pistol for escaping Helgen, a shotgun from Balgruuf, a micro SMG from Farengar for the Dragonstone, an AK-47 for Dragon Rising and a rocket launcher from the Greybeards, and the bosses of Embershard Mine, Bleak Falls Barrow, Halted Stream Camp and Silent Moons Camp give one each; the help box says what and why. Rewards go to San Andreas through the existing `GiveWeapon` command, or into the saved loadout while it isn't running. Walking over a dead body takes its gold and quest items, and E on a body or an unlocked container does the same with no menu (an owned container's gold is stolen, with Skyrim's steal alarm); the rest stays for Skyrim's menus. San Andreas's money display now counts up and down to the gold as San Andreas's does, and quest items show in the help box ("Golden Claw taken."). Skyrim's inventory, barter and container menus close as they open while San Andreas has the player (F10 to use them). No protocol change. Game-free tests for the price table, offers, the menu and its key repeat, the menu's layout, the loot filter (gold and quest items only), walking over a body, the money counter and the reward table.
- Cheat codes (protocol v20). While CJ has the player, every letter key goes into San Andreas's cheat string and San Andreas's own matching (its CRC keys of the newest letters, so its alternative codes such as FULLCLIP work too) finishes a code, with San Andreas's "Cheat activated" (or "Cheat deactivated") in the help box. HESOYAM fills Skyrim's health bar and CJ's armour, gives 250,000 gold and mends CJ's vehicle; BAGUVIX stops the player taking any damage, San Andreas's or Skyrim's; the three weapon sets come as the weapon commands' `GiveSet`; FULLCLIP is infinite ammo (the muzzle flash now counts shots from the bullets when the rounds don't go down); every vehicle cheat of a kind that spawns (Rhino, Hunter, Hydra, Vortex, Stuntplane, Quad, Monster, Dozer, Tanker without its trailer, the stock cars, Romero, Stretch, Trashmaster, Caddy) is a spawn request, as are the jetpack and the parachute; flying cars (San Andreas's own flight model, with AhShitRim's extra air control off), huge bunny hop, perfect handling, nitro, smash'n'boom, mega jump, blowing up every vehicle, fat, muscle and skinny CJ, maximum stamina, driving skills and hitman in every weapon go to San Andreas as a new `Cheat` message, found in San Andreas's own tables by key; SZCMAWO is Wasted. M no longer opens Skyrim's map while CJ has the player, since codes have it in the middle (UZUMYMW): the map is in Tab's menu, as San Andreas's is in its pause menu. NOMERCY, our own cheat, takes essential protection off every actor but the player (the actor bases once and every loaded actor as it shows up) and gives exactly those back when typed again; it is kept in the co-save in a record of its own (`ASCH`), and it can break quests. Ped, traffic, gang, weather and time cheats are left out. San Andreas's toggles are sent again when the link comes back and are all off after a load, as in San Andreas. Our own besides: MINIGUN gives a minigun, PAINT resprays and mends CJ's vehicle (its model sent again in the new colours), LOWRIDER fits San Andreas's hydraulics to the car he's in, and typed again there takes them off (the vehicle slot's new `kBodyHydraulics` says which; H raises and lowers it, the new numpad 5 key with the numpad's arrows bounces it, `kButtonHydraulics`), and every vehicle spawns by its model name from the player's own `vehicles.ide` (SANCHEZ, HYDRA, MAVERICK, NRG500: the top row's digits count). Being hit while BAGUVIX is on no longer trains the player's Block, Light Armor and Heavy Armor. Every code, its aliases and the vehicle names are in the new `docs/CHEATS.md`. Game-free tests for San Andreas's keys, the table, the typing, the M key rule, the switches and NOMERCY's bookkeeping and record.
- Every vehicle, the jetpack and the parachute (protocol v19). Any of San Andreas's land and air vehicles can be spawned on the dev path: `;` picks one (an NRG-500, a BMX, a quad, a monster truck, the Rhino, the Hunter, the Hydra, the Sparrow, the Maverick, the Dodo, the Stuntplane, the Vortex, the jetpack or the parachute, or your own list of model ids in `[Debug] Vehicles`) and `'` spawns it; `[Debug] VehicleKeys=0` turns them off. Helicopters and planes fly with San Andreas's own controls, Q and E are the rudder, the numpad's 8 and 2 (or Page Up and Page Down) swing the Hydra's nozzles between hovering and forward flight, and Caps Lock or H works the landing gear. The mouse buttons fire a vehicle's weapons: the Hunter's guns and rockets and the Hydra's missiles fly in Skyrim as CJ's own, and the Rhino's shell is a fast rocket from its muzzle, so they hurt Skyrim's people. Rotors swap between their standing blades and the spinning disc, and a door or bonnet San Andreas swaps for its damaged version is swapped in Skyrim too. The Rhino gets the four road wheels a side San Andreas draws between its front and back wheels. The Rhino's cannon booms as it fires, a spinning rotor or propeller is a see-through disc, and the Stuntplane no longer shows its low-detail body through the cockpit glass. A model with only a few back-to-back faces (the canopy) is drawn two-sided again, so its panels no longer go missing from below. Boats, trains, trailers and RC vehicles are refused with the reason in the help box. The jetpack is San Andreas's own (Shift up, Space down), drawn on CJ's back; carrying the parachute CJ takes it out and skydives off anything high enough (W dives, S slows, A and D turn), the left mouse button opens the chute, he floats down and lands, and the parachute is used up, all after San Andreas's parachute script, which never runs here. Skyrim's own fall damage no longer lands on top of San Andreas's. San Andreas's ground chunks now reuse the collision model of the map model each one borrows instead of taking one from San Andreas's pool, which the emptied map already nearly fills: past about 90 chunks around CJ (a long helicopter flight) it ran out and San Andreas crashed. A crash now logs the module it happened in. Ground is now built up to 175 m ahead of a fast vehicle (6 chunks, 2 s at a Hydra's speed), though Skyrim itself only has its land's collision about 1 s ahead at that speed, so the Hydra is marked experimental: `[Debug] StreamTest=<m/s>` measures that (it loads the latest save, goes to Riverwood and flies the player in a straight line, logging frame times and how far ahead the land is loaded). The vehicle slots carry a mask of hidden parts and a new gear slot carries the jetpack or the canopy, so the ring offsets moved. Game-free tests for the vehicle kinds of all 212 models and the seat table, rotors, the dev picks, the parachute, the input's new keys and the lookahead; `scripts/fake-vehicles.ps1` rides, flies and fires them against a running San Andreas.
- A Linux build with the real MSVC under Wine: `scripts/wine/setup-msvc-wine.sh` installs msvc-wine, the Windows SDK and vcpkg, and `scripts/wine/build.sh` builds both plugins and runs the doctest suite, the link's tests included, under Wine (about 26 minutes cold, a minute or two incremental). See `docs/DEV_SETUP.md`, "Building on Linux with msvc-wine", for the timings and known gaps.
- The spray can and the fire extinguisher work in Skyrim. Their puffs (`CWeapon::FireAreaEffect`) are sent like the flamethrower's, as flame `Shot`s told apart by the weapon type (no protocol change). The spray can is a short, wide cone that makes everyone in it choke and cough (damage, a stagger every 2 s, the player the aggressor). The extinguisher is a longer cone that makes people cough without hurting or angering them, and puts out whoever burns in it (the Molotov's and flamethrower's afterburn and any fire spell on them), the Molotov's burning oil it reaches, San Andreas's own fires along it and CJ himself. Both show San Andreas's own clouds (its spraycan and extinguisher particles, read from your install like the vehicles' smoke), with a little white puff where a fire goes out. Game-free tests for the cones, what the extinguisher reaches and the clouds' particles.
- Skyrim's people react like San Andreas's peds, with no plugin file (vanilla AI started at runtime). A civilian CJ points a gun at (San Andreas's aim ray through its crosshair, in sight, 45 m or 100 m through a scope) cowers with vanilla's cowering idle and stands up 3 s after he looks away; one that can't (sitting, at work) runs. Every shot is noise: civilians within the weapon's hearing range (pistols and SMGs 45 m as in San Andreas, heavier guns 60 to 80 m, blasts 90 m; the silenced pistol only 4 m, or 45 m for someone facing CJ and seeing him) flee from the player outside combat, and the player makes a detection event at the weapon's sound level, so bandits come looking. Someone cornered in town who would keep fleeing has its AI reset once the scare is over. The running chainsaw sends civilians within 6 m running, 15 m while it cuts. To Skyrim a gun is magic: each shot is a spell cast from the player (Skyrim's spell cast event, so `OnSpellCast` and anything else watching the player's magic sees it), no crime, and people say Skyrim's own lines about it in their own voices with lip sync, shown as San Andreas's subtitles while they play: the nearest guard "Whoa, whoa, whoa! Watch the magic!", a guard CJ aims at "Go cast your fancy magic someplace else.", a civilian he aims at "Is that magic? Careful, please...". Followers, anyone fighting or hostile, the foolhardy and creatures carry on; a brave civilian doesn't cower but still runs. `[Reactions] Enabled=0` turns it off. Game-free tests for who reacts, the hearing ranges, what counts as magic, the aim ray and cone, how long a cower and a flee last and the lines' pacing. San Andreas's subtitles look more like San Andreas's now: near white with a drop shadow instead of an outline, and no fade in or out.
- Vehicle damage you can see (protocol v18). San Andreas's damage stages now show on its vehicles in Skyrim: under 650 health the engine smokes with San Andreas's own smoke (its `overheat_car` white and black smoke: the `bullethitsmoke` sprite read from the San Andreas install's `effectsPC.txd`, drawn facing the camera with San Andreas's rates, lives, sizes, fades and drift, the white thinning out as the black thickens), under 250 it catches fire (a tall, narrow vanilla fire at the engine, four small fires stacked, with the black smoke going on) and San Andreas blows it up 5 s later. The blow-up is a big vanilla fireball that hurts and throws Skyrim's people and CJ within 9 m like a rocket (San Andreas plays the bang; CJ inside is killed by San Andreas), the wreck turns scorched black, short the wheel San Andreas knocks off, and burns while San Andreas's own fire on it lasts. The vehicle slots carry the health, wrecked and burning flags and the engine's place. Game-free tests for the stages, the smoke, the engine's place, the scorching and the blow-up.
- F11 takes a screenshot with everything on screen: San Andreas's HUD, text styles, crosshairs and scopes drawn by the overlay, and Skyrim's own HUD and menus, which Skyrim's Print Screen leaves out. - At 6 stars the reinforcements fought each other instead of CJ: the giants, the mammoth and the dragon weren't on the guards' side. - The wanted level's reinforcements could be copies of a named character, such as four Commander Caiuses at 4 stars in Whiterun: guards were copied from any guard of the hold about, and he counts as one. Only generic guards are copied now, never a unique or named NPC.
- Quickloading (F9) after a fight with the wanted level's reinforcements could crash Skyrim. The police created, disabled and set actors on each other from inside the player's update, while Skyrim was updating other actors' AI, which left a freed actor in someone's AI. All of that now runs at Skyrim's safe point between frames, anyone fighting a pursuer stops before it's taken out, and a load leaves ours to Skyrim's own clearing.
- After a giant's club or a mammoth knocked CJ down, the guards, soldiers, giants and mammoths kept swinging at the empty spot where he was hit while he stood elsewhere: Skyrim's hidden player stayed down there. It now stays down no longer than CJ (3 s), is then made to get up and put back where CJ is, and whoever was fighting it before the knock-down and dropped it is set on it again for a few seconds, so the chase goes on.
- Walking over a weapon pickup no longer swaps CJ's gun for it: as in San Andreas, when he carries another gun of its kind the help box offers the swap and E makes it (Skyrim's activation stays out of it). A shotgun, SMG or rifle pickup first tops up the one he has, and a swap keeps its rounds; a swapped handgun goes with its rounds, as in San Andreas. The same gun, or an empty slot, is still taken by walking in, its rounds on top.
- Every model San Andreas sends gets an id of its own: two built in one tick of its clock could share one, and Skyrim drew the second as the first (a pistol pickup as a heart). The jetpack put on again after it was dropped is drawn again (only its flames showed).
- The skydive eases into its falling speed as San Andreas's own fall does, instead of a constant pull up to it, and goes twice as fast ahead, so a base jump clears the mountainside before the chute opens.

- Bartering as CJ could leave the player stuck in the dialogue: picking a merchant's barter topic a second time left the conversation's camera, letterbox and cursor up with no topics and no way out, F10 included. Game-free tests for when to close and when to bring the dialogue back.
- CJ's aim looks and behaves like San Andreas's. Holding the left mouse button without aiming fires straight ahead at chest height standing still too, not into the sky (from the hip a shot always goes the way his chest faces, level). One-handed and twin guns point their barrels at the aim point instead of 15 to 20 degrees below it, and his arms reach towards it with the elbow slightly bent instead of locked straight; from the hip both arms point ahead of his chest and meet there, mirrored (twin guns no longer both lean off to his right).
- Fast travel no longer fails as CJ with "you can't fast travel while jumping or falling" while he stands still. Skyrim's hidden player, put at his feet every frame, often counted as falling; it now stands whenever CJ stands on his feet or sits in a vehicle on the ground or the water.
- A vehicle pushed through the ground in a hard crash no longer drives on under it, and CJ getting out there no longer falls. - The ground no longer stops working under CJ now and then, letting him or his car fall through the same spot again and again until the fall rescue gave up. San Andreas's collision store still streams the emptied map's collision by where CJ is in San Andreas, and the map models the ground chunks borrow were still its: loading an area wrote the map's own collision over a chunk, and unloading it emptied the chunk. The chunks' models are now taken out of the store, and a chunk emptied anyway is filled again at once. Some of the map's models also share one collision model, and two chunks borrowing such a pair overwrote each other every frame, so one chunk's place held the other's ground and a car fell through it at speed: a chunk now only borrows a model whose collision model is its alone, and never takes one from San Andreas's nearly full pool. Static triangles reaching more than 256 m from their chunk (mountain and cliff faces) are cut and their near parts kept instead of dropped whole, which left holes in rocks by the road. Under the land where San Andreas has no ground over him, CJ or his vehicle goes back where he last stood instead of onto the land's height, where he fell straight through again every 0.7 s. - CJ no longer ends up stuck for good after dying in a vehicle (driving off the Throat of the World, or a car blowing up with him in it).
