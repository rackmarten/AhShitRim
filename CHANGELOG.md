## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Quests as San Andreas missions, and the line AhShitRim is named after (protocol v23). A quest starting (once it has a journal entry, as Skyrim announces it) shows its name as San Andreas's mission title, bottom right in gold, and a quest completed shows "MISSION PASSED!" and "RESPECT +" with San Andreas's own mission passed tune, instead of Skyrim's quest notifications, whoever has the player. Objectives and failed quests stay Skyrim's. A Miscellaneous quest done (a favour, a delivery, A Lovely Letter) is San Andreas's odd job: MISSION PASSED! alone with the jingle, no RESPECT + and no title when it starts, so fetch quests don't spam; Skyrim only reports their objectives, so one counts once its objective is completed and the quest ends right after. `[Missions] MiscPassed=0` in `AhShitRim.ini` turns that off; the launcher and `deploy-local.ps1` now keep that and `KeyLayout` when they rewrite the ini. They come one at a time: a passed mission before the next one's title, and only once its tune is over. On a new game, right after Ralof's "Hey, you. You're finally awake." in the Helgen cart, CJ says "Ah shit, here we go again." in his own voice, the first line of San Andreas's intro voice-over, with San Andreas's subtitle. Both sounds are San Andreas's own, played by its own routines and ids from your install (the tune as its mission scripts play it, the line as its intro script does), through a new sound cue counter in the input; San Andreas is heard for them even while Skyrim has the player, and Skyrim's music pauses meanwhile. Game-free tests for which quests count, the order and timing, the intro's trigger and the cues.
- The wanted level, mounted guards and Busted (protocol v22). Skyrim's bounty stays the real one; on top of it San Andreas's wanted stars (0 to 6) show in its HUD while CJ has the player. They come from heat, as San Andreas's from its chaos points (its thresholds, 50 to 2,400, with the sixth star at 3,600 so it takes as much as the fifth): each crime adds some (an assault 60, a murder 100, lesser crimes 15), so shooting a guard is one star, two guards killed two, four three, and six stars take a long rampage. Out of every pursuer's sight for 5 s they start to flash, then go one at a time (8 s for the last star, 18 s for the sixth); the bounty stays, and a guard who fights CJ later brings back a star, two for a murderer. Killing the witnesses wipes Skyrim's bounty but not the stars; paying the fine, Busted, Wasted and AEZAKMI end them, and petty crimes alone are a star at most. From 2 stars the hold's guards round about join, and the guards never run out: a director keeps up to 12 of ours after CJ, one per cooldown, never in view (behind him, or out of the city's barracks door), with a breather when they're all dead: riders on saddled horses from 3 stars, a captain and a guard wizard from 4, the hold's soldiers and a dragon from 5, two giants and a mammoth at 6 (the dragon, giants and mammoth only in the open world), all of them after CJ alone rather than each other. Guards hit CJ by the stars: a quarter of their damage at 1 star, half at 3, all of it from 4. CJ says San Andreas's own chased and arrested lines. Stopped in a car for a second with a guard at the driver's door, CJ is dragged out with San Andreas's jacked animation; stopped on a bike, bicycle or quad, he's knocked off. On foot Skyrim's guard dialogue stays (the fine, resisting, persuading), and only "go to jail" changes. Every Busted plays San Andreas's "busted", takes the bounty out of the gold (all of it if short), clears it, and CJ comes to outside the hold's barracks (Dragonsreach in Whiterun) with his weapons, no jail; nobody shoots him once he's arrested. OSRBLHH (TURNUPTHEHEAT) adds two stars, LJSPQK (BRINGITON) makes it six and ASNAEB (TURNDOWNTHEHEAT) clears them. A giant's club or a mammoth throws CJ as a car's blast does (San Andreas's fall and get-up) instead of leaving Skyrim's hidden player stuck in its ragdoll with the camera frozen. AEZAKMI (IDOASIPLEASE) clears every bounty and calls the guards off at once, then keeps the stars at 0 and puts back any bounty a crime adds. The stars are saved in a co-save record of their own (`ASWL`). New input flag `kInputBusted`.
- The people CJ kills come back. Any unique NPC the player kills while San Andreas has it (Ammu-Nation's blacksmiths, the main quest's people up to the Greybeards, anyone named) gets up an in-game hour later at their editor spot, inventory reset, once their body and that spot are out of CJ's sight. Works with NOMERCY too. The dead waiting are kept in the co-save (`ASRV`). Game-free tests for whose death counts, the timing, line of sight and the record.
- Conversations as San Andreas cutscenes (protocol v21). While CJ has the player, Skyrim's dialogue slides San Andreas's letterbox bars in and cuts to a two-shot of CJ and the speaker, then to the speaker over CJ's shoulder (pulled in by walls), keeping to one side of the two all conversation. CJ answers in his own voice, through San Andreas's audio: he greets when the dialogue opens, says a line of the topic's category (greet, agree, refuse, ask, threaten, joke, goodbye) whenever you pick one, cutting back to the two-shot, and says goodbye when you leave, never the same line twice in a row. Skyrim's subtitles, in dialogue and out of it, are drawn in San Andreas's subtitle style instead of Skyrim's, and a skipped line's goes at once. E or the left mouse button skips the speaker's line, and E on someone sitting (the Jarl on his throne) talks to them instead of offering the seat. The topic list stays Skyrim's. Which category a topic is comes from a table of Skyrim.esm topic IDs (the main quest up to the Greybeards and the generic town topics, classified by hand), generated by `protocol/tools/topic-table.mjs` from your own Skyrim.esm (it can also ask the homelab's local LLM through 8-ball), and a rule on the topic's text for anything else. CJ's lines per category start as his calm lines of the nearest San Andreas speech contexts; the voice picker's new "talk" menu picks them by ear. Game-free tests for the table, the rule, noticing a pick, the cuts and the shots.
- Skyrim's people have more to say about CJ's "magic", all vanilla lines in their own voices: guards have five lines when they hear shooting and five when CJ aims at them, civilians with no guard about remark on the spell cast near them, civilians aimed at pick from the magic greetings and a couple everyone has, the flamethrower brings the remarks on playing with fire, and the minigun, rockets and blasts are taken for a shout (the reactions to the player's Thu'um). Each line is said only by voice types that have its voice file, and nobody repeats a line until they've said the others that fit them.
- Ammu-Nation, gun rewards and looting without menus: guns, ammo and armour without the debug key. While San Andreas has the player, E on the blacksmith at Warmaiden's in Whiterun (Adrianne Avenicci or Ulfberth War-Bear), or the main blacksmith of Solitude, Windhelm, Riften, Markarth, Dawnstar or Falkreath, opens San Andreas's shop menu instead of Skyrim's dialogue (the help box says "E: Ammu-Nation (Adrianne Avenicci)"). It lists San Andreas's weapons by slot (pistols, shotguns, sub-machine guns, assault rifles, rifles, heavy weapons, thrown) and body armour, everything for sale at once and paid in gold: a pistol is 150, the minigun 15,000. A gun CJ already has sells its ammo instead, for less, in green; what he can't afford is grey. W/S (or the arrows) browse, E or the sprint key chooses, F or Enter goes back and leaves; CJ stands still meanwhile, and walking off closes it. The main quest gives guns, heavier as it goes: a pistol for escaping Helgen, a shotgun from Balgruuf, a micro SMG from Farengar for the Dragonstone, an AK-47 for Dragon Rising and a rocket launcher from the Greybeards, and the bosses of Embershard Mine, Bleak Falls Barrow, Halted Stream Camp and Silent Moons Camp give one each; the help box says what and why. Rewards go to San Andreas through the existing `GiveWeapon` command, or into the saved loadout while it isn't running. Walking over a dead body takes its gold and quest items, and E on a body or an unlocked container does the same with no menu (an owned container's gold is stolen, with Skyrim's steal alarm); the rest stays for Skyrim's menus. San Andreas's money display now counts up and down to the gold as San Andreas's does, and quest items show in the help box ("Golden Claw taken."). Skyrim's inventory, barter and container menus close as they open while San Andreas has the player (F10 to use them). No protocol change. Game-free tests for the price table, offers, the menu and its key repeat, the menu's layout, the loot filter (gold and quest items only), walking over a body, the money counter and the reward table.
- Cheat codes (protocol v20). While CJ has the player, every letter key goes into San Andreas's cheat string and San Andreas's own matching (its CRC keys of the newest letters, so its alternative codes such as FULLCLIP work too) finishes a code, with San Andreas's "Cheat activated" (or "Cheat deactivated") in the help box. HESOYAM fills Skyrim's health bar and CJ's armour, gives 250,000 gold and mends CJ's vehicle; BAGUVIX stops the player taking any damage, San Andreas's or Skyrim's; the three weapon sets come as the weapon commands' `GiveSet`; FULLCLIP is infinite ammo (the muzzle flash now counts shots from the bullets when the rounds don't go down); every vehicle cheat of a kind that spawns (Rhino, Hunter, Hydra, Vortex, Stuntplane, Quad, Monster, Dozer, Tanker without its trailer, the stock cars, Romero, Stretch, Trashmaster, Caddy) is a spawn request, as are the jetpack and the parachute; flying cars (San Andreas's own flight model, with AhShitRim's extra air control off), huge bunny hop, perfect handling, nitro, smash'n'boom, mega jump, blowing up every vehicle, fat, muscle and skinny CJ, maximum stamina, driving skills and hitman in every weapon go to San Andreas as a new `Cheat` message, found in San Andreas's own tables by key; SZCMAWO is Wasted. M no longer opens Skyrim's map while CJ has the player, since codes have it in the middle (UZUMYMW): the map is in Tab's menu, as San Andreas's is in its pause menu. NOMERCY, our own cheat, takes essential protection off every actor but the player (the actor bases once and every loaded actor as it shows up) and gives exactly those back when typed again; it is kept in the co-save in a record of its own (`ASCH`), and it can break quests. Ped, traffic, gang, weather and time cheats are left out. San Andreas's toggles are sent again when the link comes back and are all off after a load, as in San Andreas. Our own besides: MINIGUN gives a minigun, PAINT resprays and mends CJ's vehicle (its model sent again in the new colours), LOWRIDER fits San Andreas's hydraulics to the car he's in, and typed again there takes them off (the vehicle slot's new `kBodyHydraulics` says which; H raises and lowers it, the new numpad 5 key with the numpad's arrows bounces it, `kButtonHydraulics`), and every vehicle spawns by its model name from the player's own `vehicles.ide` (SANCHEZ, HYDRA, MAVERICK, NRG500: the top row's digits count). Being hit while BAGUVIX is on no longer trains the player's Block, Light Armor and Heavy Armor. Every code, its aliases and the vehicle names are in the new `docs/CHEATS.md`. Game-free tests for San Andreas's keys, the table, the typing, the M key rule, the switches and NOMERCY's bookkeeping and record.
- Every vehicle, the jetpack and the parachute (protocol v19). Any of San Andreas's land and air vehicles can be spawned on the dev path: `;` picks one (an NRG-500, a BMX, a quad, a monster truck, the Rhino, the Hunter, the Hydra, the Sparrow, the Maverick, the Dodo, the Stuntplane, the Vortex, the jetpack or the parachute, or your own list of model ids in `[Debug] Vehicles`) and `'` spawns it; `[Debug] VehicleKeys=0` turns them off. Helicopters and planes fly with San Andreas's own controls, Q and E are the rudder, the numpad's 8 and 2 (or Page Up and Page Down) swing the Hydra's nozzles between hovering and forward flight, and Caps Lock or H works the landing gear. The mouse buttons fire a vehicle's weapons: the Hunter's guns and rockets and the Hydra's missiles fly in Skyrim as CJ's own, and the Rhino's shell is a fast rocket from its muzzle, so they hurt Skyrim's people. Rotors swap between their standing blades and the spinning disc, and a door or bonnet San Andreas swaps for its damaged version is swapped in Skyrim too. The Rhino gets the four road wheels a side San Andreas draws between its front and back wheels. The Rhino's cannon booms as it fires, a spinning rotor or propeller is a see-through disc, and the Stuntplane no longer shows its low-detail body through the cockpit glass. A model with only a few back-to-back faces (the canopy) is drawn two-sided again, so its panels no longer go missing from below. Boats, trains, trailers and RC vehicles are refused with the reason in the help box. The jetpack is San Andreas's own (Shift up, Space down), drawn on CJ's back; carrying the parachute CJ takes it out and skydives off anything high enough (W dives, S slows, A and D turn), the left mouse button opens the chute, he floats down and lands, and the parachute is used up, all after San Andreas's parachute script, which never runs here. Skyrim's own fall damage no longer lands on top of San Andreas's. San Andreas's ground chunks now reuse the collision model of the map model each one borrows instead of taking one from San Andreas's pool, which the emptied map already nearly fills: past about 90 chunks around CJ (a long helicopter flight) it ran out and San Andreas crashed. A crash now logs the module it happened in. Ground is now built up to 175 m ahead of a fast vehicle (6 chunks, 2 s at a Hydra's speed), though Skyrim itself only has its land's collision about 1 s ahead at that speed, so the Hydra is marked experimental: `[Debug] StreamTest=<m/s>` measures that (it loads the latest save, goes to Riverwood and flies the player in a straight line, logging frame times and how far ahead the land is loaded). The vehicle slots carry a mask of hidden parts and a new gear slot carries the jetpack or the canopy, so the ring offsets moved. Game-free tests for the vehicle kinds of all 212 models and the seat table, rotors, the dev picks, the parachute, the input's new keys and the lookahead; `scripts/fake-vehicles.ps1` rides, flies and fires them against a running San Andreas.
- A Linux build with the real MSVC under Wine: `scripts/wine/setup-msvc-wine.sh` installs msvc-wine, the Windows SDK and vcpkg, and `scripts/wine/build.sh` builds both plugins and runs the doctest suite, the link's tests included, under Wine (about 26 minutes cold, a minute or two incremental). - The spray can and the fire extinguisher work in Skyrim. Their puffs (`CWeapon::FireAreaEffect`) are sent like the flamethrower's, as flame `Shot`s told apart by the weapon type (no protocol change). The spray can is a short, wide cone that makes everyone in it choke and cough (damage, a stagger every 2 s, the player the aggressor). The extinguisher is a longer cone that makes people cough without hurting or angering them, and puts out whoever burns in it (the Molotov's and flamethrower's afterburn and any fire spell on them), the Molotov's burning oil it reaches, San Andreas's own fires along it and CJ himself. Both show San Andreas's own clouds (its spraycan and extinguisher particles, read from your install like the vehicles' smoke), with a little white puff where a fire goes out. Game-free tests for the cones, what the extinguisher reaches and the clouds' particles.
- Skyrim's people react like San Andreas's peds, with no plugin file (vanilla AI started at runtime). A civilian CJ points a gun at (San Andreas's aim ray through its crosshair, in sight, 45 m or 100 m through a scope) cowers with vanilla's cowering idle and stands up 3 s after he looks away; one that can't (sitting, at work) runs. Every shot is noise: civilians within the weapon's hearing range (pistols and SMGs 45 m as in San Andreas, heavier guns 60 to 80 m, blasts 90 m; the silenced pistol only 4 m, or 45 m for someone facing CJ and seeing him) flee from the player outside combat, and the player makes a detection event at the weapon's sound level, so bandits come looking. Someone cornered in town who would keep fleeing has its AI reset once the scare is over. The running chainsaw sends civilians within 6 m running, 15 m while it cuts. To Skyrim a gun is magic: each shot is a spell cast from the player (Skyrim's spell cast event, so `OnSpellCast` and anything else watching the player's magic sees it), no crime, and people say Skyrim's own lines about it in their own voices with lip sync, shown as San Andreas's subtitles while they play: the nearest guard "Whoa, whoa, whoa! Watch the magic!", a guard CJ aims at "Go cast your fancy magic someplace else.", a civilian he aims at "Is that magic? Careful, please...". Followers, anyone fighting or hostile, the foolhardy and creatures carry on; a brave civilian doesn't cower but still runs. `[Reactions] Enabled=0` turns it off. Game-free tests for who reacts, the hearing ranges, what counts as magic, the aim ray and cone, how long a cower and a flee last and the lines' pacing. San Andreas's subtitles look more like San Andreas's now: near white with a drop shadow instead of an outline, and no fade in or out.
- Vehicle damage you can see (protocol v18). The vehicle slots carry the health, wrecked and burning flags and the engine's place. Game-free tests for the stages, the smoke, the engine's place, the scorching and the blow-up.

- Swear keys, as in My Summer Car (protocol v16): X has CJ shout one of San Andreas's own insults in his voice and give the finger (ped.ifp's `FUCKU`) on foot, or just shout out of the window in a vehicle; the middle mouse button has him swear to himself (his crash exclamations), no gesture. San Andreas's 7 to 8 s wait before a line can play again is skipped for both, so every press is heard. The input's spare bytes at 84 and 90 are now `insultCount` and `swearCount`. Game-free tests for the counters and the picks' deck; `scripts/fake-skyrim.ps1` checks the finger moves his arm.
- Explosives and fire (protocol v15): CJ's rockets, grenades, tear gas and Molotovs fly in Skyrim. San Andreas still throws and fires them (its animations, aim, force and fuse), but each one it makes for CJ (`CProjectileInfo::AddProjectile`) is sent to Skyrim as a `Projectile` message and taken out of San Andreas's empty world without a blast. Skyrim flies it under San Andreas's gravity with the bullets' ray tests, draws it with San Andreas's model, bounces grenades and tear gas until their fuse runs out, and sets them off with its own effects: a vanilla fireball explosion for rockets and grenades that hurts everyone within 6 to 7 m through the bullets' hit and damage table, throws people around (not giants, mammoths or dragons) and opens locked doors and chests within 3 m (a crime if someone sees, as picking them would be; key-only locks stay shut); a fireball twice that size and burning oil on the ground for a Molotov, spreading from the middle to about 3 m over 10 s and burning 30 s, setting whoever it catches or stands in it on fire for 6 s more; a gas cloud for tear gas that chokes and staggers everyone in it for 15 s. The flamethrower's puffs (`CWeapon::FireAreaEffect`) come as a new flame `Shot`: Skyrim shoots the Flames spell's flames from the nozzle and burns everyone in its cone, and San Andreas's own creeping fire is skipped for CJ. CJ is hurt by his own blasts, fire and gas, armour first. Explosives hurt in full whatever the target's level or size (about five rockets for a mammoth). The sounds are San Andreas's own: Skyrim's explosions are silent copies, and a new `Effect` message back to San Andreas plays its explosion sound and camera shake, a rocket's roar in flight, a Molotov's bottle smashing and its fire's crackle; CJ caught in a Molotov's fire is set alight by San Andreas (his scream and burn), shown with Skyrim's flames on him (`kPlayerOnFire` in the pose). Game-free tests for the flight, the bounce, the blast falloff and knock, the lock rule, the fire patches, the gas and the flame cone, and drawing a projectile's parts.
- The game-free protocol tests build and run on Linux: a `linux-tests` CMake preset (Ninja, gcc or clang, doctest fetched by CMake instead of vcpkg) and `scripts/test.sh`, which CI also runs in a quick `protocol-tests-linux` job on Ubuntu next to the Windows build. The link's tests stay Windows-only.
- Guns hurt (protocol v13): every bullet and shotgun shot CJ fires, the sniper rifle's shot and every melee blow at its hit frame go to Skyrim as a `Shot` message on the ring (San Andreas's line after its own spread, the weapon's reach and damage; a blow's centre, radius and `melee.dat` damage), caught in San Andreas at `CWeapon::FireInstantHit`'s line tests, `CWeapon::DoBulletImpact`, `CWeapon::FireSniper` and `CTaskSimpleFight::FightStrike` (San Andreas's aspect ratio, which its minimized window left at 0, is set to 4:3 so its third-person aim gives a line at all). Skyrim fans a shotgun's shot out into its pellets and ray tests each bullet through its Havok world: statics, furniture, the land and water stop it, it passes the player, the dead and clutter, the first living actor takes it, and a line through the head bone's sphere is a headshot. The first shot of a burst goes down the crosshair, and the spread grows back to San Andreas's as the fire button is held; the aim circle is San Andreas's size and sits where the shots go. A blow lands once on each actor it reaches (two and a half times its `melee.dat` damage) and staggers by chance; the chainsaw keeps cutting, 150 a second, and staggers on every cut. Blood shows on actors and Skyrim's impact effect and sound where a bullet stops (stone's where the shape has no material). The pose grew again, so the offsets after it moved. Game-free tests for the damage table, the shotguns' pellets, body parts, races, headshots, level and size scaling, the melee reach, blows and chainsaw cuts and the skill hits; `scripts/fake-weapons.ps1` also checks every gun sends its shots and every melee weapon its blows, and that hits raise a skill.
- CJ's weapons (protocol v12): San Andreas's weapons in his hands. San Andreas keeps them (its slots, ammo, switching, animations, aiming and firing); the mouse wheel switches weapons on foot (each step one press of its next or previous weapon button, steps that come together one after another), the right button aims (its lock-on and free aim, and the sniper rifle's, rocket launchers', M4's and camera's first-person modes; CJ isn't drawn through the sniper, rocket and camera ones) and the left one fires or attacks. The weapon in his hand is sent as a model once (every drawn part of its clump a bone, as the vehicles are) and placed on his hand bone every frame the way San Andreas does when it draws a ped, so Skyrim draws it posed in his hand like CJ, twin pistols in both hands and the minigun's barrels turning; San Andreas's gun task is fired by calling the step of drawing a ped that fires it, since hidden nothing does. - Health, Wasted and CJ in the save (protocol v11): Skyrim's health is the only health. San Andreas keeps CJ at a fixed full health with a buffer, so nothing there can kill him, and reports the damage it works out for him (falls, crashes, explosions) as a running total; Skyrim takes it off its player, CJ's armour (the HUD's bar, 0 to 100) first. When the player's health runs out it bleeds out (it is essential while CJ has it) and Wasted plays: slow motion in both games, the picture draining to grey, "wasted", a fade to black, then the player is taken to the temple of the hold it fell in (Kynareth, Mara, the Divines, Talos or Dibella; the small holds and the wilds the nearest), wakes at full health 100 gold lighter (all of it if short) with its weapons, and the screen fades back in. While Unbound (the Helgen intro) runs the player stays mortal and Skyrim reloads the last save as usual. A new SKSE co-save record holds whether San Andreas had the player, CJ's armour and the car he was in; saving, quicksaving and loading restore CJ, the handover resumes by itself, and the car is put back under him with its colours once the ground is in. The pose now carries the vehicle CJ is in and the damage, the input the dying flag and a loaded save's car; the pose and audio slots grew, so every offset after them moved. ### Fixed

- CJ no longer ends up stuck for good after dying in a vehicle (driving off the Throat of the World, or a car blowing up with him in it). Now San Andreas never runs its own Wasted: its game logic never sees CJ dead, he stays dead in his seat as San Andreas plays it while Skyrim's Wasted runs, and is revived when it is over.
