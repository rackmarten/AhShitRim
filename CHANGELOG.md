# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Windows CI on GitHub Actions: every pull request and push to `main` builds both plugins and runs the game-free tests, and uploads the built `AhShitRim.dll`/`.pdb` and `AhShitRim.asi`/`.map` as an artifact. `AGENTS.md` tells agents without a compiler or the games how to work against it.
- San Andreas's HUD in Skyrim: while CJ has the player, Skyrim draws San Andreas's HUD top right (fist icon, clock on Skyrim's game time, health bar on the player's health, money on their gold, armour bar and wanted stars once there are any) and hides its own compass, attribute bars and crosshair until F10. San Andreas's text styles are there for later work: the mission title, "wasted", "busted", "mission passed!", the help box, subtitles, fade to black and letterbox bars; `/` shows each in turn. Everything is drawn in San Andreas's own fonts and sprites, read at startup from the install in `AhShitRim.ini` (written by `scripts/deploy-local.ps1`), never written to disk, by a new `Present` overlay (DirectXTK `SpriteBatch`), keeping 4:3 proportions held to the screen's edges on 16:9, 16:10 and 21:9. Game-free tests for the texture dictionaries, `fonts.dat`, San Andreas's letter layout and the HUD layout; with `SaPath` in `local.props`, `scripts/test.ps1` also reads the real install's files.
- Vehicles (protocol v6): ], [ and `\` in Skyrim spawn a Sandking, a ZR-350 or a Sanchez in front of CJ, parked across his path on the first flat, clear spot of 15 tried on Skyrim's ground; up to four at once. F or Enter gets in and out with San Andreas's own animations, W/S/A/D drive, Space is the handbrake, H the horn, and San Andreas's own vehicle physics (and its rule throwing a rider off a bike on a hard hit) run on Skyrim's ground. San Andreas sends each vehicle's model once, every drawn part as a bone in the vehicle's own colours (wheel scale and mirroring baked in), and every part's frame every frame in one of four new vehicle slots, after running the vehicle's `PreRender` itself so the wheels spin, steer and sit on their suspension and a bike's forks, handlebars and lean move. Skyrim draws the vehicles like CJ, and the chase camera is San Andreas's own. A new handover takes CJ out of his vehicle and removes them all. Game-free tests for the spawn spot rule, part baking, mesh merging, paint colours and the vehicle keys; `scripts/fake-skyrim.ps1` now spawns all three, drives the Sandking (47 m in 3 s), rides the Sanchez and checks a new handover clears them.
- The fall rescue also catches the vehicle CJ is in, and the vehicles move with the floating origin.
- In a vehicle the up and down arrows lean a bike forward and back (San Andreas's keyboard layout), for wheelies, stoppies and clearing bumps; on foot they still walk. A spawned bike stands on its side stand.

### Changed

- Skyrim's ground in San Andreas is tarmac everywhere (was San Andreas's default surface), so cars and bikes grip like on a road.
- Skyrim samples the ground up to 3 chunks ahead of the player (was 2), with twice the time per frame above 10 m/s, retries an incomplete chunk after 0.4 s at speed (was 2 s), and San Andreas keeps chunks up to 7 from CJ (was 6), so the road ahead of a fast car is in in time.
- Skyrim multiplies textured meshes by their material colour (a vehicle's paint) and draws glass blended; `CjView` is now `ModelView` and draws any number of models.

### Fixed

- San Andreas crashed after the runner's 5 s stats once the vehicles came in: `CPed::ShoulderBoneRotation` is a static cdecl function that plugin-sdk calls as a member, which left the stack 4 bytes off and a register of CJ's frame code wrong. It is called with its real convention now.
- The floating origin moving (1 km from the handover spot, never reached in game before) stopped a fast car dead and rolled it: the wheels' last contact points stayed behind in world space. They move with the vehicle now, and `scripts/fake-skyrim.ps1` drives 1.3 km through an origin move.
- A bike leaned twice as far as CJ in turns: San Andreas's `PreRender` already leans the parts, and the lean was put on the root as well.
- A read of the input slot that stayed torn counted as the link going down, which handed the player back to Skyrim for a frame (and would have spawned the last vehicle again); the last input now stands for that frame.

- CJ in Skyrim (protocol v5): San Andreas sends CJ's model once over a new ring back to Skyrim (his skinned geometry, skin weights and textures, read from the running game: nothing shipped or written to disk), and his 32 bones and its camera every frame in a new body slot. San Andreas animates CJ in full while hidden and poses his bones itself, shoulders included (`CPed::ShoulderBoneRotation`, which only runs while drawing). CJ is dressed in his vest, jeans and sneakers, as the intro script does. Skyrim builds the model in memory, skins it on the CPU into dynamic vertex buffers every frame, draws CJ at the player's place with the player's own body hidden, and puts its camera on San Andreas's third-person camera (position, direction and field of view), all smoothed between San Andreas's 30 frames a second. Skyrim's sneak eye is hidden while CJ stands, so it no longer stays up after a crouch. First person isn't supported yet. Game-free tests for the model blob, mesh building, texture decoding, the bone math and the interpolation; `scripts/fake-skyrim.ps1` also checks CJ's model arrives and skins to a man's height with moving bones.
- Skyrim's ground in San Andreas: while San Andreas has the player, the SKSE plugin samples Skyrim's terrain (a heightfield) and the statics' real collision around the player in 29 m chunks (PerkeleRim's collision sampler) and streams them over a new 4 MiB ring (protocol v4). San Andreas builds each chunk as an invisible collision object around CJ, under a floating origin that moves with him, drops far ones, holds CJ at the handover spot until the ground under him is in, and puts him back where he last stood if he ends up below the ground. Game-free tests for the ring, the chunk payloads, the floating origin, the chunk bookkeeping and the fall rescue; `scripts/fake-skyrim.ps1` sends flat ground and checks CJ walks on it.
- Hidden San Andreas runner: the ASI starts San Andreas windowed and minimized (`scripts/launch-sa.ps1`, `-Visible` for a small 800x600 window), keeps it simulating without the focus, skips the splashes, movies and main menu, and starts a new game without the mission script in an empty world (no map collision or IPLs, no peds, traffic or wanted level). Physics runs at 30 frames a second, nothing is rendered while hidden, and sounds are muted.
- F10 in Skyrim hands the player to San Andreas and back. While handed over, WASD, mouse look, Shift (sprint), Space (jump), C (crouch) and Alt (walk) drive CJ, and his position, heading and velocity move the Skyrim player in first person. Escape, Tab, M and the console stay Skyrim's.
- Protocol v2 and v3: an input slot (Skyrim to San Andreas) and a pose slot (San Andreas to Skyrim, with CJ's crouch, which makes Skyrim sneak), with game-free tests for the input mapping and the pose conversion.
- `scripts/restart-games.ps1` (the whole test loop in one command) and `scripts/fake-skyrim.ps1` (drives a running San Andreas without Skyrim and checks CJ walks).
- README: concept, architecture, planned features, milestone 1, requirements and asset policy.
- Scaffold: SKSE plugin (`skse/`, CommonLibSSE-NG v10.1.0, CMake + vcpkg) and 32-bit San Andreas ASI plugin (`sa/`, plugin-sdk, CMake with the Visual Studio generator). Both log when they load; the ASI refuses to start on any exe but GTA: San Andreas 1.0 US and logs why.
- Protocol v1 link ([docs/PROTOCOL.md](docs/PROTOCOL.md)): a shared memory section with magic, protocol version and a heartbeat per side. Each side logs when the other appears, disappears or speaks another version. The same link code compiles into both plugins (64-bit and 32-bit).
- Game-free unit tests for the protocol header layout and the handshake (doctest, `scripts/test.ps1`).
- `scripts/build.ps1`, `deploy-local.ps1`, `test.ps1`, `logs.ps1`; `local.props.example` for game paths.
- [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md), [docs/PROTOCOL.md](docs/PROTOCOL.md), [docs/DEV_SETUP.md](docs/DEV_SETUP.md) (Build Tools, plugin-sdk, the San Andreas downgrade and ASI loader).
