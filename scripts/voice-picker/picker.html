CJ voice picker

Every line in CJ's speech banks, by San Andreas's speech context and his mood voice. Press ▶ to listen, then mark a line X for the insult key or MMB for the swear key, or give it a category for CJ's answers in Skyrim's conversations (greet, agree, refuse, ask, threaten, joke, goodbye). Your picks are kept in this browser; download them when you're done.

In AhShitRim CJ is always in the CR mood (no wanted level, no mission just passed), so the game on its own only plays the CR lines. Your picks play whatever the mood.
